Link in bio or link in DM: the best tools for Indian creators

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our pick for Indian creators who want link in bio and link in DM together: its link-in-bio page with email capture is on every plan, alongside comment-to-DM with unlimited DMs. If you only need DM link delivery, ReplyKaro is the cheapest paid option at ₹99 a month.

"Link in bio" asks a viewer to leave the reel, open your profile, find the link and tap it. "Comment LINK" asks them to type four letters. That is why most creators now send links by DM, and keep a bio page for profile visitors.

This page compares tools by how well they do both. We focus on tools that deliver links by DM; dedicated link-in-bio page builders are not compared here because we have not checked their current plans.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Bio page and DM links in one tool

Tadlo AI includes a link-in-bio page with lead capture on every plan, so profile visitors can tap your links or subscribe with their email. Its analytics show bio link clicks, referrers and conversion next to your DM flows.

For reels, comment-to-DM sends the exact link to the person who asked, with unlimited DMs on every plan. Emails from both routes land in the same leads inbox.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

Make your bio page earn

  • Put your current offer or newest freebie first.
  • Collect emails, not only clicks.
  • Keep it to a few links; long lists get fewer taps.
  • Check which links get clicked and reorder monthly.

Make your DM links earn

  • Say the keyword in the reel, not only in the caption.
  • Put the link in the first line of the DM.
  • Use a carousel for multi-product reels.
  • Track clicks per reel.

Collecting emails on both

A DM catches the people who watched a reel; a bio page catches the people who visited your profile. Ask for an email in both places for the same freebie, and keep the list in one leads inbox, so you see all your subscribers together and know which route brought each one.

Cost in rupees

Tadlo AI's bio page and DM automation are both on the free plan; Creator is ₹249 a month. A separate bio tool plus a separate DM tool means two subscriptions and two places to update links.

Frequently asked questions

Is link in bio still worth it?

For profile visitors, yes. For reel viewers, a link in DM gets more clicks because it takes one step instead of several.

Which tool gives me a link-in-bio page and auto-DMs?

Tadlo AI includes both on every plan, including Free, with email capture on the bio page.

How many links should my bio page have?

A few. Lead with your newest offer or freebie, then your main links. Long lists get fewer taps on each link.

Can I collect emails on my link-in-bio page?

Yes, with Tadlo AI's link-in-bio page, which sends emails to the same leads inbox as your DMs.
