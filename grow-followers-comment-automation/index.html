How to grow Instagram followers with comment automation

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Grow followers by pairing a freebie people want with a clear comment CTA and a follow gate on that reel, then measuring follows per reel. Keep gates for your best freebies and soft-ask everywhere else. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: a follow gate checked through Instagram's API and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Every comment-to-DM is a moment where a viewer has already decided they like what you made. A follow gate turns that moment into a follow. Done well, it can be the single biggest growth lever a creator has.

Done badly, it feels like a toll booth. This guide is about doing it well.

The growth formula

  1. A reel that solves a small problem.
  2. A freebie that solves the next problem: the full list, the template, part two.
  3. A CTA: "Comment NOTES and follow, I'll send it."
  4. A follow gate that checks, then sends.
  5. A DM that tells them what to expect next week.

Freebies worth a follow

  • Templates and presets.
  • Full recipes or ingredient lists.
  • Study notes and cheat sheets.
  • Itineraries and packing lists.
  • A longer version of the reel.

Gate only your best

If every reel is gated, your audience notices and resents it. Gate one or two of your strongest freebies a week, and let the rest send links freely. Your followers grow from the gated ones, and your goodwill grows from the rest.

Why verification matters for growth

If you are running a gate to grow, the people who tap "I'm following" without following are the ones you are trying to reach. Tadlo has published production data from its follow gate: across 12,344 follow checks over 23 days, 41% of commenters asking for a link did not follow the account, and 33.3% of people who tapped "I'm following" were not actually following. A gate that checks catches them.

Keep new followers

  • Post the promised part two on time.
  • Reply to story reactions from new followers.
  • Do not follow up a gated DM with sales messages.

Measure follows, not comments

Comments are cheap; follows are the goal. For each gated reel, compare people asked with people who followed. Reels with a high follow rate show you what your audience values.

When growth stalls

  • Your freebie may not be valuable enough; try a template instead of a tip.
  • Your CTA may come too late in the reel; move it earlier.
  • You may be gating too often; drop to one gated reel a week.

Stay within the rules

Only DM people who commented or messaged you, vary your public replies, and use a tool on Meta's official API. Growth tactics that spam strangers are how accounts get limited.

Frequently asked questions

Can comment automation grow my Instagram followers?

Yes. A follow gate on reels with a strong freebie turns commenters into followers.

How often should I use a follow gate?

On one or two of your strongest freebies a week, not on every reel.

What is a good CTA for follower growth?

A specific freebie plus the keyword and the follow ask: "Follow and comment NOTES, I'll send them."

Does a follow gate reduce how many people get my freebie?

A little, because some people do not follow. Use hard gates for strong freebies and soft asks elsewhere.

Will followers from gates unfollow later?

Some will, which is normal. Keep them by delivering what you promised and posting the part two or next freebie on time.

Do fake follows matter?

Yes, if growth is the goal. A verified gate catches people who tap "I'm following" without following.
