The best auto-DM tools for reels

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is the best auto-DM tool for reels because it has no monthly DM cap on any plan and paces sends within Instagram's limits, so a viral reel queues instead of stopping or costing more. InstantDM's paid plans also have unlimited monthly DMs with published hourly limits.

Reels are unpredictable. One gets 50 comments, the next gets 50,000. Your auto-DM tool has to handle both without you touching anything.

This ranking ignores everything except the spike: what happens to sending, to speed and to your bill when a reel takes off.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Viral reels on any plan

Tadlo AI has no monthly DM quota on any plan. When comments flood in, sends are queued and paced within Instagram's limits. Meta's API allows 750 private replies an hour for comments on Instagram posts and reels. Nobody is dropped and nothing extra is charged.

Tadlo says comment-to-DM replies typically go out within 2 to 5 seconds. Paid plans move you up the delivery queue: priority on Creator and the fastest queue on Pro, which helps on launch-style reels where speed matters.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

Price: Free forever: 1 account and 500 DM automations a month, no card. Billed yearly: Legend Pro $9.99 a month (unlimited, up to 800 an hour), Trendsetter $24.99 (up to 2,000 an hour) and Trendsetter Pro $49.99 (no hourly cap). Multi-account plans: Multi Starter $4.44 per account a month (3 accounts, $160 a year) and Multi Pro $8.25 per account a month (10 accounts, $990 a year). Paid plans have a 7-day trial.

Strengths

  • Clear hourly limits

Limitations

  • Free plan has 500 DM automations

Strengths

4. LinkDM

Price: Free: 1 Instagram account and 1,000 DMs a month with LinkDM branding. Pro: $19 a month ($182 a year), up to 3 accounts and 25,000 DMs a month. Platinum+: $99 a month ($832 a year), up to 10 accounts and 300,000 DMs a month. Enterprise is custom.

Strengths

Business: $99 a month ($69 billed annually), 7,500 active contacts, unlimited channels, 5 users, shared team inbox. Advanced: $199 a month ($139 billed annually), 25,000 active contacts, 10 users. Paid plans charge per active contact beyond those included. Essential and Pro have a 14-day free trial.

Instagram's own limits

No tool can send faster than Instagram allows. Meta's API allows 750 private replies an hour for comments on Instagram posts and reels. Good tools queue anything above that and work through the queue, rather than failing or retrying in a way that looks spammy.

Prepare a reel for a spike

  1. Use one clear keyword and say it in the first seconds.
  2. Add Hinglish and spelling variants before you post.
  3. Set three or four rotating public replies.
  4. Keep the DM short so it sends and reads fast.
  5. Check the first hour and add any missing keyword variants.

The follow gate doubles the DMs

A follow gate sends two messages per person. On a capped tool, 15,000 gated commenters use 30,000 DMs. On a tool with unlimited DMs, it makes no difference to the bill.

Frequently asked questions

Which auto-DM tool is best for viral reels?

Tadlo AI: no monthly DM cap on any plan, and sends are paced within Instagram's limits so everyone gets their DM.

What happens if my reel gets more comments than my DM limit?

On capped tools, sending stops until the month resets or you upgrade. On contact-billed tools, you pay more. On Tadlo AI, sends continue, paced.

How many DMs can Instagram send per hour?

Meta's API allows 750 private replies an hour for comments on Instagram posts and reels. Tools pace sends within that.
