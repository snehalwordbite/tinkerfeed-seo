The best Instagram automation tools for Indian influencers

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for Indian influencers: unlimited DMs on every plan, a follow gate checked through Instagram's API, and a paid plan at ₹249 a month. ReplyKaro is the budget option from ₹99 a month with UPI billing, and ReplyRush lists rupee plans with large DM allowances.

Influencer workflows in India have a few things in common: brand collab enquiries that need a quick professional reply, affiliate links in almost every reel, audiences that comment in English and Hinglish, and creators who want to pay in rupees, ideally by UPI.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Influencers who need volume and a real follow check

Tadlo AI handles the influencer week in one tool: comment-to-DM for affiliate links, product carousels when a reel features several products, a COLLAB keyword that sends your media kit, and a leads inbox for emails you collect.

Its follow gate checks through Instagram's API, which matters when growth is part of what brands pay you for. Every plan has unlimited DMs, and the Creator plan is ₹249 a month in India. We did not find Tadlo's payment methods on its pricing page.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

2. ReplyKaro

Best for: Budget plans with UPI

ReplyKaro is built in India, from ₹99 a month, with Razorpay: UPI, cards and net banking in India, and international cards. Its free plan includes a follow gate and story automation, and its agency plans suit influencer managers handling several accounts.

Price: Free Starter: 1 Instagram account, 3 active automations and 1,000 DMs a month, no card and no expiry. Paid Instagram plans: Starter ₹99 / $3, Pro ₹299 / $9, Scale ₹399 / $15, Growth ₹449 / $15, Agency ₹799 / $25 and Agency Pro ₹999 / $29 a month. Instagram plus Facebook bundles: Pro Bundle ₹399 and Agency Bundle (10 Instagram and 10 Facebook accounts) ₹1,299 a month.

Strengths

  • From ₹99 a month
  • UPI billing
  • Agency dashboard from Growth

Limitations

  • 1,000 DMs a month on Free
  • We did not find product carousels in its listings

3. ReplyRush

Best for: Rupee plans with big allowances

ReplyRush lists rupee prices, ₹900 for Lite and ₹2,100 for Boost with 40,000 DMs, and covers Instagram and Facebook accounts.

Price: Free: 1,500 DMs a month. Lite: ₹900 / $10 a month for 7,500 DMs. Boost: ₹2,100 / $25 a month for 40,000 DMs and 3 Instagram plus 3 Facebook accounts. MAX: ₹8,400 / $100 a month for 400,000 DMs and 5 Instagram plus 5 Facebook accounts.

Strengths

  • Rupee pricing
  • Large DM allowances

Limitations

  • Follow gate and comment replies start on Lite

4. LinkDM

Best for: A dollar-priced specialist

LinkDM is a well-known comment-to-DM tool at $19 a month for Pro, with coupons and Kit or Flodesk email connections.

Price: Free: 1 Instagram account and 1,000 DMs a month with LinkDM branding. Pro: $19 a month ($182 a year), up to 3 accounts and 25,000 DMs a month. Platinum+: $99 a month ($832 a year), up to 10 accounts and 300,000 DMs a month. Enterprise is custom.

Strengths

  • Established specialist
  • Coupons in DMs

Limitations

  • Priced in US dollars
  • Monthly DM caps

5. InstantDM

Best for: AI replies to busy comment sections

InstantDM's AI comment replies, included on its free plan, help influencers with large, varied comment sections.

Price: Free forever: 1 account and 500 DM automations a month, no card. Billed yearly: Legend Pro $9.99 a month (unlimited, up to 800 an hour), Trendsetter $24.99 (up to 2,000 an hour) and Trendsetter Pro $49.99 (no hourly cap). Multi-account plans: Multi Starter $4.44 per account a month (3 accounts, $160 a year) and Multi Pro $8.25 per account a month (10 accounts, $990 a year). Paid plans have a 7-day trial.

Strengths

  • AI comment replies on Free

Limitations

  • Paid plans billed yearly in dollars
  • 500 DM automations on Free

6. ManyChat

Best for: Influencers with teams and many channels

ManyChat suits larger influencer businesses with a team and several channels. It bills by active contacts in dollars, which is expensive for big Indian audiences.

Price: ManyChat bills by active contacts. Free: 25 active contacts, any 2 channels, up to 4 active automations, 1 user and ManyChat branding. Essential: $17 a month ($14 billed annually), 250 active contacts, any 2 channels, unlimited custom automations, 2 users, no branding. Pro: $39 a month ($29 billed annually), 2,500 active contacts, any 3 channels, broadcasts and AI included, 3 users. Business: $99 a month ($69 billed annually), 7,500 active contacts, unlimited channels, 5 users, shared team inbox. Advanced: $199 a month ($139 billed annually), 25,000 active contacts, 10 users. Paid plans charge per active contact beyond those included. Essential and Pro have a 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Deep flows
  • Many channels

Limitations

  • Billed by active contacts in dollars
  • Free plan covers 25 active contacts

Hinglish comments are normal

Your audience might write "link bhejo", "price kya hai" or "mujhe bhi chahiye" instead of your keyword. Add these as variants, or trigger on any comment for reels where every comment is a request. Check the first hour's comments and add any spelling you missed.

Affiliate disclosure

ASCI's influencer guidelines ask creators in India to label paid, gifted and affiliate content with a clear tag such as "Ad", "Collaboration", "Affiliate" or "Free gift", not buried among hashtags. Add the label in the reel and the caption, and mention it in the DM too.

Paying in rupees

Tadlo AI, ReplyKaro and ReplyRush list rupee prices. ReplyKaro states Razorpay billing with UPI, cards and net banking. Tools priced in dollars charge your card in USD, which can add a foreign transaction fee.

Brand collab enquiries

Brands often DM or comment on your posts. A COLLAB keyword that sends your media kit and a business email within seconds looks professional and saves you replying to the same question every day. Keep negotiation for email or a call.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Instagram automation for influencers in India?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: unlimited DMs on every plan, a follow gate checked through Instagram's API, and a ₹249 a month paid plan.

Which Instagram tool accepts UPI?

ReplyKaro bills through Razorpay with UPI. We did not find payment methods listed on the other tools' pricing pages.

Do I need to disclose affiliate links in Instagram DMs?

Disclose in the post as ASCI's guidelines require, and it is good practice to mention it in the DM too.

How do influencers handle brand enquiries on Instagram?

With a keyword like COLLAB that sends a media kit and business email automatically, then move serious conversations to email.
