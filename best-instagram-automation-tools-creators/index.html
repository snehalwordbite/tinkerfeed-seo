The best Instagram automation tools for creators

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is the best Instagram automation tool for creators: unlimited DMs on every plan, so a viral reel never hits a cap, and a follow gate that checks through Instagram's API whether people really follow you. ReplyKaro is the cheapest paid option in India, and LinkDM is the long-running specialist.

If you make reels, you have seen it: "comment LINK and I'll send it" gets ten times the comments of "link in bio". Comment-to-DM is now the default way creators deliver links, freebies and affiliate products, and grow followers at the same time.

So the tool question is really about three things: what happens when a reel takes off, whether the follow request actually works, and what you get without paying. We ranked six tools on exactly those, using each tool's published plans.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Creators whose reels go viral

Tadlo AI is built around the creator use case. Every plan, Free included, has unlimited DMs and automations, so the reel that gets 20,000 comments costs you the same as the one that gets 200. Sends are paced within Instagram's limits, so a spike queues instead of failing.

The follow gate is the other big reason. Tadlo has published production data from its follow gate: across 12,344 follow checks over 23 days, 41% of commenters asking for a link did not follow the account, and 33.3% of people who tapped "I'm following" were not actually following. A gate that only trusts the "I'm following" tap misses that third; Tadlo's checks through the API.

You also get story reply automation, email capture into a leads inbox, a link-in-bio page and AI reel analysis, 5 a month on Free. The paid Creator plan is ₹249 a month in India.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

2. ReplyKaro

Best for: The cheapest paid plan in India

ReplyKaro is popular with Indian creators for its price: paid plans from ₹99 a month, billed through Razorpay with UPI. Its free plan includes a follow gate and story automation, limited to 3 active automations and 1,000 DMs a month.

Price: Free Starter: 1 Instagram account, 3 active automations and 1,000 DMs a month, no card and no expiry. Paid Instagram plans: Starter ₹99 / $3, Pro ₹299 / $9, Scale ₹399 / $15, Growth ₹449 / $15, Agency ₹799 / $25 and Agency Pro ₹999 / $29 a month. Instagram plus Facebook bundles: Pro Bundle ₹399 and Agency Bundle (10 Instagram and 10 Facebook accounts) ₹1,299 a month.

Strengths

  • Paid plans from ₹99 a month
  • Follow gate and stories on Free
  • UPI billing

Limitations

  • 1,000 DMs a month on Free
  • How its follow check works is not published

3. LinkDM

Best for: A long-running comment-to-DM specialist

Price: Free: 1 Instagram account and 1,000 DMs a month with LinkDM branding. Pro: $19 a month ($182 a year), up to 3 accounts and 25,000 DMs a month. Platinum+: $99 a month ($832 a year), up to 10 accounts and 300,000 DMs a month. Enterprise is custom.

Strengths

  • Simple and dependable
  • Story replies and mentions on Free

Limitations

  • Monthly DM caps
  • Follower Growth Tool on paid plans only

6. ManyChat

Best for: Creators who also use other channels

Business: $99 a month ($69 billed annually), 7,500 active contacts, unlimited channels, 5 users, shared team inbox. Advanced: $199 a month ($139 billed annually), 25,000 active contacts, 10 users. Paid plans charge per active contact beyond those included. Essential and Pro have a 14-day free trial.

What creators actually automate

  • Link delivery: "Comment LINK" sends the product, the freebie or the YouTube video.
  • Follower growth: a follow request before the link.
  • Email list: asking for an email in the DM before sending a guide.
  • Brand enquiries: "Comment COLLAB" or a DM keyword sends your media kit.
  • Story replies: "Reply YES" on a story sends the link.

The viral reel test

Every tool looks fine on a quiet week. The test is the reel that suddenly gets 15,000 comments. On a tool with a monthly DM cap, sending stops partway and the latest commenters get nothing. On a tool that bills per contact, the bill jumps that month. On a tool with unlimited DMs that paces sends, everyone gets their DM, a little slower at the peak, at no extra cost.

Comment keyword ideas

Short, easy to type, and said out loud in the reel: LINK, GUIDE, RECIPE, PDF, SHOP. If your audience types Hinglish, add variants such as "link do", "bhejo" and "chahiye". Our keyword ideas guide has lists by niche.

Safety, briefly

All six tools use Meta's official APIs. The risks come from behaviour: posting the same public reply hundreds of times, or messaging people who did not ask. Vary your public replies, only DM people who commented, and no tool can promise your account will never be limited, because Meta makes the final call.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Instagram automation tool for creators?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: unlimited DMs on every plan, a follow gate checked through Instagram's API, and story replies and email capture on the free plan.

Which Instagram automation tool is cheapest in India?

Tadlo AI's free plan has unlimited DMs. Among paid plans, ReplyKaro starts at ₹99 a month.

Do I need a Facebook Page to automate my Instagram DMs?

Not with Tadlo AI, which connects through Instagram Login. You need a creator or business account.

Can automation get my Instagram account banned?

Tools on Meta's official API are permitted. Avoid spammy behaviour, such as identical public replies at scale, and use a tool that paces sends.
