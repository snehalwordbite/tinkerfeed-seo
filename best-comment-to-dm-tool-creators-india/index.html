The best comment-to-DM tools for Indian creators

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is the best comment-to-DM tool for Indian creators: it can trigger on any comment, so Hinglish variants are covered, checks the follow through Instagram's API, and has unlimited DMs on every plan. ReplyKaro is the cheapest paid choice at ₹99 a month.

The "comment LINK" workflow is everywhere on Indian Instagram: a reel ends with the keyword, the comments fill with LINK, link do, bhejo and a hundred emojis, and every commenter expects a DM within seconds.

That workflow has three Indian-specific problems: keyword spellings, follow gates people tap without following, and reels that blow up overnight. We ranked tools on those.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: The comment LINK workflow at any volume

Tadlo AI can trigger on any comment on a post, which is the easiest fix for Hinglish spellings: everyone who comments gets the DM. Or you can list keywords and variants, with several rules on the same reel.

The follow gate checks through Instagram's API rather than trusting the button, and you choose a soft ask or a hard gate. Unlimited DMs on every plan means the reel that hits a million views does not hit a wall.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

Keyword or any comment?

For reels where every comment is a request, trigger on any comment and stop worrying about spelling. For reels where people also chat, use a keyword plus variants so you do not DM someone who just wrote "nice".

Hinglish keyword variants to add

Variants Indian audiences type
KeywordVariants to add

PRICEprice kya hai, kitne ka, rate, prize, kitna
GUIDEguide do, pdf, notes, bhejo
SHOPkaha se liya, where to buy, kaha milega

Follow gates people actually complete

Ask once, explain why ("I post one of these every week"), and give a button to check again. A gate that verifies through the API catches people who tap "I'm following" without following. Tadlo has published production data from its follow gate: across 12,344 follow checks over 23 days, 41% of commenters asking for a link did not follow the account, and 33.3% of people who tapped "I'm following" were not actually following.

Public replies in the comment section

Rotate a few short public replies such as "Check DM", "Bhej diya" and "Sent!". Identical replies hundreds of times look like spam.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best comment-to-DM tool in India?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: any-comment triggers for Hinglish spellings, a follow gate checked through the API, and unlimited DMs on every plan.

How do I handle Hinglish comments in auto-DM?

Trigger on any comment, or add Hinglish variants like "link do" and "bhejo" to your keyword list.

Should I trigger on any comment or a keyword?

Any comment for reels where every comment is a request; a keyword plus variants for reels where people also chat, so you do not DM someone who only wrote "nice".

Which comment-to-DM tool is cheapest in India?

Tadlo AI is free with unlimited DMs. ReplyKaro's paid plans start at ₹99 a month.
