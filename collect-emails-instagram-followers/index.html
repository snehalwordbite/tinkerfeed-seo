How to collect emails from your Instagram followers

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Offer a specific freebie, ask for the email inside the DM before sending it, and keep every address in one place with the post it came from. Add the same offer to your link-in-bio page. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: email capture in DMs and on its bio page on every plan, with a leads inbox and CSV export.

Your followers live on Instagram's terms: reach changes, accounts get hacked, platforms shift. An email list is the part of your audience you own. It is also how creators sell courses, products and paid communities.

This guide shows how to turn DMs into subscribers without feeling pushy.

Why email still matters

  • You can reach subscribers without an algorithm deciding.
  • Emails convert better for launches than posts do.
  • You keep the list if anything happens to your account.

What to offer

  • The full version of what the reel showed.
  • A template, checklist or preset.
  • A free mini-course or challenge.
  • Early access to your next drop or batch.

How to ask in a DM

Ask before you deliver, and say what they will get: "Drop your email and I'll send the full guide, plus one tip every week. Unsubscribe any time." Clear wording gets fewer, better subscribers, and fewer complaints.

Consent and respect

Tell people they will get emails, send what you promised, and include an unsubscribe link in every email. Rules differ by country. In the UK, the ICO says you must not send marketing emails to individuals without specific consent, apart from a limited soft opt-in for your own previous customers.

Where emails go

Store emails with the date and the post they came from. Export them to your email platform regularly. On Tadlo AI, export is 100 leads on Free and unlimited on paid plans.

The first email

  1. Send the freebie straight away, even if the DM already did.
  2. Introduce yourself in two lines.
  3. Say what you will send and how often.
  4. Ask one question they can reply to.

What to send after the first email

  • A weekly tip that builds on the freebie.
  • Behind the scenes of what you are making next.
  • Early access to a product, course or batch, once you have one.
  • A question that invites a reply, so subscribers feel heard.

Keep the rhythm you promised in the DM. If you said weekly, do not send daily.

Common mistakes

  • Collecting emails and never writing to them, until the list goes cold.
  • Asking for the email after sending the freebie, when people have no reason to give it.
  • Hiding the fact that you will email them again.
  • Keeping the list only inside one tool and never exporting it.

Measure your list

Track subscribers per reel and per route. Reels with high views but few sign-ups need a better freebie; reels with fewer views but many sign-ups are worth repeating.

Frequently asked questions

How do I collect emails from Instagram?

Ask for the email inside the DM before sending a freebie, and add email capture to your link-in-bio page.

Can I export emails collected on Instagram?

Yes. Tadlo AI exports to CSV: 100 leads on Free, unlimited on paid plans.

Is it legal to collect emails through DMs?

Yes, if people know what they are signing up for and can unsubscribe easily. Check the rules where your audience lives.

How many emails should I expect from a reel?

It varies a lot by niche and freebie. Track sign-ups per reel for a month to learn your own baseline, then test better freebies against it.

What freebie gets the most emails?

Something specific to the reel: the full recipe, the template or the notes. Vague offers convert poorly.
