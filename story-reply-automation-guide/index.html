Instagram story auto-reply: a creator's guide

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Story auto-reply sends a DM when someone replies to your story, often with a keyword like "reply YES". Because they messaged you, it opens Instagram's 24-hour window, so you can also ask a follow-up. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; story replies are on every plan, including Free, with unlimited DMs.

Stories reach your most engaged followers, the ones who tap through every day. A story reply is them starting a conversation with you, which makes it one of the warmest triggers on Instagram.

This guide shows how to set up story auto-replies and what to post to get them.

Set it up

  1. In your tool, create a story reply automation.
  2. Choose whether it fires on any reply or on a keyword.
  3. Write the DM: the link or freebie, plus one question if you want to learn more.
  4. Post the story with a clear prompt: "Reply YES for the list".
  5. Test from a second account.

Story ideas that get replies

  • "Reply GUIDE and I'll send the full version."
  • A poll, then "reply if you want the winning recipe".
  • "Reply EARLY for first access to the drop."
  • A question sticker answered with a link to a longer post.

Use the 24-hour window

Instagram lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message (up to 7 days after a click-to-Direct ad), and a person on your team can reply within a separate 7-day human agent window. Because a story reply comes from them, your automation can send the link and ask one follow-up question, such as their email or what they want next.

Keyword or any reply?

Use any reply for stories that are clearly a call to action. Use a keyword when your stories also get casual replies, so a friend saying "haha" does not get your freebie.

Story mentions

When someone tags you in their story, some tools can send a thank-you DM. It is a small touch that brings people back.

When to reply by hand

Reply personally to anything personal or any question the automation cannot answer. People who reply to your stories are often your most loyal followers, and a human answer now and then keeps them that way.

Story replies vs comments: when to use which

Use reels and comment keywords to reach new people. Use stories to serve the people who already follow you: early access, part twos, behind-the-scenes freebies. Followers who reply to stories are often your best customers later, so treat those conversations well.

Measure it

Track replies per story and links clicked. Stories that get many replies show what your core audience wants, which is useful for planning reels too.

Frequently asked questions

Can I auto-reply to Instagram story replies?

Yes. Tadlo AI, ReplyKaro, LinkDM, CreatorFlow and ReplyRush include it on their free plans.

What should I put in a story to get replies?

A clear prompt with a reason: "Reply YES for the full list" or "Reply EARLY for first access".

Can I ask follow-up questions after a story reply?

Yes. A story reply opens Instagram's 24-hour messaging window, so the automation can ask one or two questions.

Can I run story automation and comment automation together?

Yes. They are separate triggers, and Tadlo AI uses the same flow builder for both.

How long should a story auto-reply be?

Short: the link or freebie in the first line and, at most, one question. People reply to stories on the go.

Do story reactions trigger automations?

Story replies and reactions open a DM conversation. Check which events your tool supports.
