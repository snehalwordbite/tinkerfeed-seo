The best tools for affiliate creators on Instagram

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is the best tool for affiliate creators on Instagram because a comment can trigger a carousel of 2 to 10 products in one DM, every plan has unlimited DMs, and its analytics show clicks per flow. LinkDM is a simple option for one link plus a coupon, and InstantDM adds AI replies for busy comment sections.

Affiliate creators live on one number: clicks on product links. Every reel ends with "comment LINK", and every comment is a potential commission. The tool you use decides how many of those comments become clicks.

We ranked tools on what affiliate creators need: several products per reel, fast delivery, click tracking, and no cap on the reel that does best.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Several affiliate products per reel

Comment-to-product carousels send 2 to 10 product cards in one DM, on every plan including Free. So a "my skincare routine" reel can send all five products, each with its affiliate link, in one DM.

The analytics dashboard is free on every plan and shows DMs, link clicks and flow performance, so you can see which reels earn. A/B testing lets you compare two versions of the DM to find the one that gets more clicks. Unlimited DMs mean a viral reel never stops sending links.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

The affiliate reel formula

  1. Show the product in use in the first seconds.
  2. Say the keyword: "Comment LINK for everything I used".
  3. The DM sends a carousel of every product with its affiliate link.
  4. Disclose in the reel, the caption and the DM.
  5. Check clicks per reel and repeat what works.

Disclosure is not optional

ASCI's influencer guidelines ask creators in India to label paid, gifted and affiliate content with a clear tag such as "Ad", "Collaboration", "Affiliate" or "Free gift", not buried among hashtags. A short line in the DM, such as "These are affiliate links, I may earn a commission", keeps you honest with your audience too.

Track clicks, not comments

Comments show interest; clicks show intent; orders pay. Use your affiliate platform's link tracking and your DM tool's click analytics together, and judge reels by clicks and earnings, not by comment counts.

Amazon and brand links

Affiliate links from marketplaces and brand programmes work the same way in a DM. Keep links short and clearly labelled, and check them often; expired links are the most common reason a good reel stops earning.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best tool for sending affiliate links on Instagram?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: product carousels of 2 to 10 items in one DM, click analytics, and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Can I send several affiliate links in one Instagram DM?

Yes, as a product carousel. Tadlo AI sends 2 to 10 product cards in one DM on every plan, including Free.
