The best free auto-DM tools in India

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI has the best free auto-DM plan for Indian creators: unlimited DMs and automations, with a follow gate and story replies, for free. ReplyKaro's free plan is next, with a follow gate and stories but a 1,000-DM monthly limit and 3 automations; its paid plans start at ₹99.

Most creators start on a free plan, and most free plans quietly stop working on your best reel. The limit is the thing to compare: some count DMs, some count automations, some count contacts.

Here is what each free plan gives you, and what the first upgrade costs in rupees if you need it.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: A free plan with no DM limit

Tadlo AI's free plan is free forever with no card: unlimited DMs and automations for one Instagram account, comment-to-DM, story replies, DM keywords, the follow gate, email capture and a link-in-bio page.

The catches, stated plainly: DMs carry a "via Tadlo" line, lead export stops at 100 leads, and it covers one account. The Creator plan at ₹249 a month removes the branding and export limit.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

2. ReplyKaro

Paid Instagram plans: Starter ₹99 / $3, Pro ₹299 / $9, Scale ₹399 / $15, Growth ₹449 / $15, Agency ₹799 / $25 and Agency Pro ₹999 / $29 a month. Instagram plus Facebook bundles: Pro Bundle ₹399 and Agency Bundle (10 Instagram and 10 Facebook accounts) ₹1,299 a month.

Limitations

  • 1,000 DMs a month

Price: Free: 1,500 DMs a month. Lite: ₹900 / $10 a month for 7,500 DMs. Boost: ₹2,100 / $25 a month for 40,000 DMs and 3 Instagram plus 3 Facebook accounts. MAX: ₹8,400 / $100 a month for 400,000 DMs and 5 Instagram plus 5 Facebook accounts.

Strengths

How fast free limits run out

A follow gate sends about two DMs per person: the follow request and the link. So a reel with 600 commenters uses about 1,200 DMs, already past a 1,000-DM free plan. If your reels regularly get a few hundred comments, a plan without a DM cap saves you from upgrading mid-month.

Free does not mean unsafe

These free plans run on Meta's official APIs, the same as the paid ones. Stay away from free tools that ask for your Instagram password; those are the ones that put accounts at risk.

When to upgrade

  • You want your own name on DMs instead of the tool's branding.
  • You need more than one account.
  • You want to export a list of more than 100 leads (on Tadlo AI).
  • You hit a monthly DM or automation limit on another tool.

Frequently asked questions

Which auto-DM tool is completely free in India?

Tadlo AI's free plan has unlimited DMs and automations for one account, with no card. ReplyKaro's free plan allows 1,000 DMs a month.

Is there a free follow-to-get-link tool?

Yes. Tadlo AI and ReplyKaro include a follow gate on their free plans; Tadlo's checks through Instagram's API.

What is the cheapest paid auto-DM plan in India?

ReplyKaro, from ₹99 a month. Tadlo AI's paid plan is ₹249 a month.

Do free auto-DM tools add branding?

Often, yes. Tadlo AI adds a "via Tadlo" line on Free, and LinkDM adds its branding on Free.
