What auto-DM tools cost Indian creators

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Most Indian creators can pay nothing: Tadlo AI's free plan has unlimited DMs, and ReplyKaro's free plan covers 1,000 DMs a month. Paid plans start at ₹99 a month on ReplyKaro and ₹249 on Tadlo AI; ReplyRush starts at ₹900. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job because its free plan has no DM limit.

"How much does auto-DM cost?" depends less on the price list than on your reels: how many people comment, whether you use a follow gate, and how many accounts you run.

Here are the rupee prices, and how to work out which plan you actually need.

What pushes you onto a paid plan

  • A monthly DM cap on your free plan.
  • A follow gate, which doubles the DMs per person.
  • Wanting your own branding instead of the tool's.
  • A second Instagram account.
  • Features kept for paid plans, such as email capture on some tools.

Work out your monthly DMs

  1. Count comments on your busiest reel last month.
  2. Double it if you use a follow gate.
  3. Add story replies and DM keywords.
  4. Compare with each free plan's limit.

Dollar-priced tools

LinkDM, InstantDM and CreatorFlow price in US dollars. Your card is charged in USD, and your bank may add a foreign transaction fee. Tadlo AI, ReplyKaro and ReplyRush list rupee prices.

Paying with UPI

ReplyKaro bills through Razorpay: UPI, cards and net banking in India, and international cards. We did not find payment methods listed on the other tools' pricing pages.

A worked example

Take a creator whose best reel last month got 2,500 comments, with a follow gate on that reel. That is about 5,000 DMs in a month, five times ReplyKaro's free limit and more than three times ReplyRush's. On Tadlo AI's free plan it costs nothing. A creator whose reels get a hundred comments each and who does not use a gate fits comfortably in any of the free plans.

When paying is worth it

Pay when the paid feature earns its keep: your own branding on DMs when brands are watching, more than one account when you run a second page, or a full export of your email list when you are about to launch something. Paying just because a counter ran out is the expense to avoid, which is why the DM limit is the first thing to compare.

Avoid paying twice

Some creators pay for a link-in-bio tool and a DM tool separately. Tadlo AI includes a link-in-bio page with email capture on every plan, so one tool covers both.

GST

Indian prices are often quoted excluding GST. Check the checkout total before you subscribe.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an auto-DM tool cost in India?

Free on Tadlo AI with unlimited DMs, or from ₹99 a month on ReplyKaro. ReplyRush starts at ₹900.

What is the cheapest auto-DM tool in India?

Tadlo AI's free plan costs nothing and has unlimited DMs. The cheapest paid plan we found is ReplyKaro's at ₹99 a month.

Do auto-DM tools charge in rupees?

Tadlo AI, ReplyKaro and ReplyRush list rupee prices. LinkDM, InstantDM and CreatorFlow price in dollars.

Should I pay yearly to save money?

Only once you are sure the tool suits you. Some dollar-priced tools quote their lowest price billed yearly, which means paying for a year up front.

Is GST extra on auto-DM tools?

Often, yes. Check the total at checkout.
