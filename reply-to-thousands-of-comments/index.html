How to reply to thousands of Instagram comments

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Let automation send a private reply to every comment that asks for something, rotate a handful of short public replies, and answer the interesting comments yourself. Pacing handles the volume. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; it has unlimited DMs on every plan and paces sends within Instagram's limits.

A viral reel can bring more comments in a day than you normally get in a year. Replying to each by hand is impossible, and ignoring them wastes the moment.

The trick is to split comments into three kinds and handle each differently.

Private replies at scale

A private reply to a comment is one message per comment, sent within 7 days of the comment; you can continue only if the person replies within 24 hours. Automation sends that one private reply to every requester in seconds, or queues them when the volume is high.

Public replies without looking like a bot

  • Write four or five short variants: "Sent!", "Check your DMs", "Bhej diya", "Done, enjoy".
  • Let the tool rotate them.
  • Reply publicly only to comments that triggered a DM.
  • Never post the same sentence hundreds of times.

How pacing works

Meta's API allows 750 private replies an hour for comments on Instagram posts and reels. Good tools queue anything above that rate and work through it, so the last commenters wait a little longer instead of being skipped.

What to answer by hand

  • Thoughtful questions about the content.
  • Collab and business enquiries.
  • Complaints or corrections.
  • Comments from people you know.

A viral-day routine

  1. Check that the automation is firing in the first hour.
  2. Add any keyword spellings it is missing.
  3. Spend twenty minutes replying to the best questions by hand.
  4. Pin a comment that answers the most common question.
  5. Next day, post a follow-up reel while attention is high.

Prepare before a reel can go viral

  • Set the automation before posting, not after the comments arrive.
  • Write your public reply variants in advance.
  • Decide which questions you will answer by hand.
  • Make sure your freebie or link is live and works on a phone.

Viral moments are short. Preparation means you spend them engaging, not fixing settings.

After the wave

When comments slow down, look at what people asked most and make your next reel answer it. A viral reel gives you a free survey of what your new audience wants, and following up quickly is how you keep the followers you gained.

Pin to reduce repeats

When the same question appears hundreds of times, pin a comment that answers it. Fewer people will ask, and those who do will see the answer.

When automation is not enough

If your tool has a monthly DM cap, a viral reel can exhaust it. Plan for your biggest day by choosing a tool without a monthly cap.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reply to all Instagram comments automatically?

You can send a private reply to every comment that matches your keyword, and rotate short public replies. Answer real questions by hand.

Will identical public replies get my account flagged?

They can look like spam. Rotate several short versions.

What if my DM tool cannot keep up?

Tools queue sends within Instagram's rate limits. If yours has a monthly cap, a viral reel may exhaust it.

Do I need to reply publicly to every comment?

No. Reply publicly to a sample of matching comments with rotating short replies; the private DM does the real work.

Should I like every comment?

You do not need to. Liking the best comments and replying to real questions is enough; automation covers the requests.

How many private replies can one comment get?

One. Further messages need the person to reply first.
