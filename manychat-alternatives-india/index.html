The best ManyChat alternatives for Indian creators

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is the best ManyChat alternative in India: it is free with unlimited DMs, its paid plan is ₹249 a month, and it never bills per contact. ReplyKaro is the budget pick with UPI billing from ₹99 a month, and ReplyRush lists rupee plans with large DM allowances.

ManyChat bills in US dollars by active contacts, and since 2 March 2026 its free plan covers 25 contacts. For Indian creators, whose reels can reach lakhs of people, that combination is expensive: every commenter becomes a contact billed in dollars.

These alternatives price in rupees, and most do not charge per contact at all.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Rupee pricing with no contact billing

Tadlo AI covers what Indian creators use ManyChat for on Instagram: comment-to-DM, story replies, keyword DMs, email capture and link delivery, plus product carousels and a link-in-bio page. It prices in rupees, free or ₹249 a month for Creator, and no plan charges per contact or per DM.

What it does not replace: ManyChat's other channels and its huge template library.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

2. ReplyKaro

Best for: Budget plans with UPI

ReplyKaro is built in India with plans from ₹99 to ₹999 a month, billed through Razorpay with UPI, cards and net banking.

Price: Free Starter: 1 Instagram account, 3 active automations and 1,000 DMs a month, no card and no expiry. Paid Instagram plans: Starter ₹99 / $3, Pro ₹299 / $9, Scale ₹399 / $15, Growth ₹449 / $15, Agency ₹799 / $25 and Agency Pro ₹999 / $29 a month. Instagram plus Facebook bundles: Pro Bundle ₹399 and Agency Bundle (10 Instagram and 10 Facebook accounts) ₹1,299 a month.

Strengths

  • UPI billing
  • From ₹99 a month

Limitations

  • 1,000 DMs on Free
  • Narrower feature set

3. ReplyRush

Best for: Rupee plans with large allowances

ReplyRush lists rupee prices from ₹900 a month and covers Instagram and Facebook accounts.

Price: Free: 1,500 DMs a month. Lite: ₹900 / $10 a month for 7,500 DMs. Boost: ₹2,100 / $25 a month for 40,000 DMs and 3 Instagram plus 3 Facebook accounts. MAX: ₹8,400 / $100 a month for 400,000 DMs and 5 Instagram plus 5 Facebook accounts.

Strengths

  • Rupee pricing
  • Large allowances

Limitations

  • Key features start on Lite

4. LinkDM

Best for: A simpler dollar-priced option

LinkDM is simpler and cheaper than ManyChat for comment-to-DM, at $19 a month for Pro, though still priced in dollars.

Price: Free: 1 Instagram account and 1,000 DMs a month with LinkDM branding. Pro: $19 a month ($182 a year), up to 3 accounts and 25,000 DMs a month. Platinum+: $99 a month ($832 a year), up to 10 accounts and 300,000 DMs a month. Enterprise is custom.

Strengths

  • Simple and predictable

Limitations

  • Priced in dollars
  • Monthly DM caps

5. InstantDM

Best for: Flat yearly pricing

InstantDM charges a flat rate, $9.99 a month billed yearly, with unlimited monthly DMs on paid plans.

Price: Free forever: 1 account and 500 DM automations a month, no card. Billed yearly: Legend Pro $9.99 a month (unlimited, up to 800 an hour), Trendsetter $24.99 (up to 2,000 an hour) and Trendsetter Pro $49.99 (no hourly cap). Multi-account plans: Multi Starter $4.44 per account a month (3 accounts, $160 a year) and Multi Pro $8.25 per account a month (10 accounts, $990 a year). Paid plans have a 7-day trial.

Strengths

  • Flat rate
  • AI comment replies on Free

Limitations

  • Billed yearly in dollars

Why ManyChat is expensive for Indian creators

  • Dollar billing. Your card is charged in USD, which can add foreign transaction fees.
  • Contacts, not DMs. Every person who interacts in a month is billed.
  • Indian reach. Reels in India often reach very large audiences quickly, which is exactly what contact billing charges for.
  • A 25-contact free plan, since March 2026.

Moving off ManyChat

  1. Export your contacts from ManyChat.
  2. List each live automation: post, keyword, message, link.
  3. Rebuild your busiest reel's automation first and test it.
  4. Turn ManyChat off on each post before turning the new one on.
  5. Cancel once every post has moved.

When to stay

Stay on ManyChat if you rely on other channels or on integrations already built into your flows. For Instagram-only creators, the alternatives above cover the same jobs for less.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ManyChat alternative in India?

Tadlo AI: free with unlimited DMs, ₹249 a month for its paid plan, and no per-contact billing.

Is Tadlo AI cheaper than ManyChat in India?

For Instagram-only creators, yes: Tadlo AI's free plan has unlimited DMs and its paid plan is ₹249 a month, while ManyChat bills by active contacts in dollars.

Is there a ManyChat alternative with UPI payment?

ReplyKaro bills through Razorpay with UPI. We did not find payment methods listed for the others.

Can I move my ManyChat flows to Tadlo AI?

Yes, by rebuilding them. Export your ManyChat contacts, recreate each keyword and message in Tadlo AI, and switch posts over one at a time.

Why is ManyChat expensive in India?

It bills in dollars by active contacts, and Indian reels often reach large audiences, so contact counts rise quickly.
