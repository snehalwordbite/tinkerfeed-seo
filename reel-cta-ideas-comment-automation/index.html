Reel CTA ideas that get comments

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

The best reel CTAs name a specific freebie, give one keyword, and come early: "Comment RECIPE and I'll send you the full list." Say it, show it on screen and pin it as a comment. We recommend Tadlo AI for delivering what the CTA promises; it sends the DM in seconds with unlimited DMs on every plan.

A CTA is the sentence that turns a viewer into a commenter. Most creators put a weak one at the very end, when half the viewers have already scrolled. Small changes to wording and timing can change how many people comment far more than a new tool can.

Here are the CTA patterns that work with comment-to-DM, and examples you can adapt.

The anatomy of a strong CTA

Four parts

Rewrites

When to say it

  • Early: in the first few seconds, as a hook: "I'll send you this whole list, comment LIST."
  • At the end: repeat it once.
  • On screen: as text while you say it.
  • In the caption's first line, and pinned as the first comment.

Rewards that make people comment

  • Something they can use today: a template, a list, a link.
  • Something that saves them time: the full version of a fast reel.
  • Something exclusive: early access or part two.

Hinglish CTAs

If your audience speaks Hinglish, your CTA can too: "Comment LINK, main DM kar dunga" or "Recipe chahiye? Comment RECIPE." Add the Hinglish variants people will type as keywords.

Avoid these

  • Several different CTAs in one reel.
  • A keyword that is hard to spell.
  • Promising something you have not prepared yet.
  • A CTA that asks for a follow and a share and a comment all at once.

Test your CTAs

Try two CTA wordings on similar reels and compare comments per thousand views. Keep the winner and test the next element: timing, then the reward.

CTAs for stories and carousels

Stories use a reply instead of a comment: "Reply PLAN and I'll send it." Carousel posts can put the CTA on the last slide, with the keyword large and clear. Keep the same keyword across the reel, story and carousel for the same freebie, so your audience learns it.

CTA mistakes we see most

  • Saying the keyword only in the caption, where most viewers never read.
  • Changing the keyword between the video and the caption.
  • Promising a freebie that arrives days later.
  • Ending with "let me know in the comments", which gives nothing to comment.

Make sure the DM delivers

A strong CTA with a slow or broken DM is worse than no CTA. Test the automation before posting and check the first hour's comments for keyword variants.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CTA for Instagram reels?

"Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [specific freebie]." Say it early, show it on screen and pin it.

Where should the CTA go in a reel?

Early, as part of the hook, and again at the end, with on-screen text and a pinned comment.

Does "link in bio" still work?

It works for profile visitors, but "comment LINK" usually gets far more responses from reel viewers.

How long should a CTA be?

One short sentence. Name the keyword and the reward; anything more gets skipped.

Can I write my CTA in Hinglish?

Yes, if your audience does. Add Hinglish variants to your keyword list too.
