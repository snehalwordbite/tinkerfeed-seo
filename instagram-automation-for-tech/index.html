Instagram automation for tech creators

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tech creators should send gadget and setup links by comment keyword, include key specs in the DM, disclose affiliate links and review units, and auto-reply to brand enquiries. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: product carousels for full setups, keyword DMs and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Tech audiences ask precise questions: which model, which version, will it work with my phone. A well-built DM answers the common ones, links the exact product and saves you replying to the same question hundreds of times.

This playbook covers the tech creator's setup, with extra care on disclosure, because tech audiences are quick to spot undisclosed deals.

Exact models matter

Link the exact model and version you used, not a search page. Tech products have confusing variants, and a wrong link leads to returns and lost trust.

Disclosure for tech

  • Label affiliate links in the reel, caption and DM.
  • Say when a product was sent to you for review.
  • Say when a brand paid for the video.
  • ASCI's influencer guidelines ask creators in India to label paid, gifted and affiliate content with a clear tag such as "Ad", "Collaboration", "Affiliate" or "Free gift", not buried among hashtags.

Specs in the DM

For gadget reviews, add three to five key specs and one line on who it suits: "Great for students who need long battery life; skip it if you edit video." It answers most follow-up questions before they come.

Compatibility questions

When people ask "will it work with my phone", ask for their model in the DM and answer personally, or link the maker's compatibility page. Do not let automation guess.

Keep links current

  • Tech prices and stock change quickly; check links weekly.
  • Update to newer models when the reel's product is discontinued, and say so.
  • Remove deals that have ended.

Unboxings and first impressions

Unboxing reels draw early buyers and sceptics. Send the product link with a clear note that it is a first impression, and promise a longer review only if you will deliver it. Honest first impressions keep your audience trusting the long-term verdict.

Comparison reels

"Phone A vs phone B" reels get heated comments. Send both product links in a carousel, plus a one-line verdict for each type of buyer. It helps viewers decide and keeps the comment section focused on questions rather than arguments.

Brand enquiries

Tech brands contact creators often for launches. A COLLAB keyword that sends your media kit and email keeps it organised. Agree in writing what you can say honestly before you accept a review unit.

Long-term reels

Setup tours and "best gadgets for students" reels keep getting comments for months. Keep their DMs working, update links when models change, and add a note when a newer version replaces the one in the video.

Measure

Track clicks and sales per product. Setups and "what I use" reels often earn for months, so keep their DMs working.

Frequently asked questions

How can tech creators share gadget links on Instagram?

With a comment keyword that sends the exact product link, or a carousel for full setups.

Do I need to disclose review units?

Yes. Say when a product was sent to you, and label paid content and affiliate links clearly.

What should a gadget DM include?

The exact model link, a few key specs and who it suits.

Can I share discount codes from brands?

Yes, in the DM, with clear disclosure of the relationship and the code's end date.

How do I answer compatibility questions?

Ask for the device model and answer personally, or link the maker's compatibility page.
