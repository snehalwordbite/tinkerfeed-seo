How to send affiliate links in Instagram DMs

By TinkerFeed Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Set a comment keyword on your reel that sends a DM with your affiliate links, use a carousel when the reel features several products, and disclose the affiliate relationship in the post and the DM. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: product carousels of 2 to 10 items, click analytics and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Affiliate links are how many creators get paid for recommendations, and Instagram makes them awkward: captions do not allow clickable links, and the bio holds only a few. DMs fix that. The commenter asked, so the link arrives at the right moment.

This guide covers setup, multi-product reels, tracking and the disclosure rules Indian creators need to follow.

Basic setup

  1. Get your affiliate link from the programme (marketplace or brand).
  2. Set a keyword on the reel: LINK or the product name.
  3. Write the DM: product name, one line on why you like it, the link.
  4. Add a disclosure line: "Affiliate link, I may earn a commission."
  5. Test from a second account.

Several products in one reel

Routine, haul and "what's in my bag" reels feature several products. Send a carousel with a card for each, so viewers can tap the one they want. Tadlo AI's carousel sends 2 to 10 product cards in one DM, on every plan.

Disclosure

ASCI's influencer guidelines ask creators in India to label paid, gifted and affiliate content with a clear tag such as "Ad", "Collaboration", "Affiliate" or "Free gift", not buried among hashtags. Label the reel and caption, and repeat a short line in the DM. It protects you and keeps your audience's trust.

Link hygiene

  • Check links weekly; affiliate links and product pages change.
  • Use the platform's own short links where available.
  • Swap out-of-stock products for alternatives quickly.
  • Keep one link per product per DM, so tracking stays clean.

Better DMs, more clicks

  • Put the link in the first lines; do not bury it.
  • Say one honest thing about the product.
  • Use A/B testing to compare two versions of the DM.
  • Avoid long lists of hashtags or emojis before the link.

A DM that earns

"Here's everything from my morning routine. Affiliate links, I may earn a small commission. The cleanser is the one I'd buy first." Then the carousel. The disclosure is in, the recommendation feels personal, and the links are one tap away.

Affiliate links from stories

Stories work for affiliate links too. A story with "reply LINK" opens a DM where the automation sends the link, and because the reply opens the 24-hour window, you can answer follow-up questions about the product in the same thread.

Old reels keep earning

Affiliate reels get comments for weeks. Keep the rule on, update links when products change, and you will see clicks from reels you posted months ago.

Volume and caps

A viral haul reel can bring thousands of LINK comments. On tools with monthly DM caps, the latest commenters get nothing; on tools with unlimited DMs, everyone gets the link.

Frequently asked questions

Can I send Amazon affiliate links in Instagram DMs?

Yes. A comment keyword can trigger a DM with the link. Follow the affiliate programme's own rules on how links may be shared.

How do I send several affiliate products in one DM?

Use a product carousel. Tadlo AI sends 2 to 10 product cards in one DM.

Do I need to disclose affiliate links in DMs?

Disclose in the post as ASCI's guidelines require, and add a short line in the DM too.

Is it better to send one product or many?

Match the reel. One product for a single-product review; a carousel for routines, hauls and setups.

Can I change the link after posting the reel?

Yes. Update the DM in your tool and every new commenter gets the new link, without editing the reel.

Why are my affiliate links not getting clicks?

Usually the link is buried, the DM is too long, or the link is broken. Put it first and check it regularly.
